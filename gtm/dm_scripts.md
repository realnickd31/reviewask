# DM Scripts (keep them short)

Ground rules: read each group's rules first, and don't post links in a group if self-promotion is banned. Lead with something useful, not the pitch. Only DM people who posted or commented about reviews or leads, or who own a business in your target niche.

## Facebook (business owner groups)
**Opener after they post about leads, reviews, or slow season:**
> Hey {{name}}, saw your post in {{group}}. Quick question: how many Google reviews do you get a month right now? I built a simple tool that texts customers a review link after each job. Happy to set you up free for 2 weeks if you want to test it.

**Value post for the group (if promo is allowed or it's a weekly "share your business" thread):**
> Trick that got a few local trades 20+ Google reviews in a month: text every customer within an hour of finishing the job, with the direct review link (not "find us on Google"). I automated this as ReviewAsk ($39/mo). DM me if you want a free trial. Happy to share the message template either way.

## Nextdoor (Business page / neighborhood)
Nextdoor bans unsolicited sales DMs to neighbors, so only message **local business pages**, or people who reply to your post.
> Hi, I'm Nick, a local here. I help neighborhood businesses get more Google reviews with a 1-tap text after each job. Would you want to try it free for 2 weeks? No pressure.

**Post (Business page / "local business" category):**
> Local business owners: if you're tired of asking customers for reviews in person, I built a tool that sends them a text with your Google review link after the job. Free 2-week trial for anyone in {{neighborhood}}. Comment or message me.

## LinkedIn (RE agents, dental owners, home service owners)
**Connection note (under 300 characters):**
> Hi {{name}}, I work with local {{niche}} owners on getting more Google reviews. I'd like to connect.

**After they accept:**
> Thanks for connecting, {{name}}. Quick one: is getting Google reviews something you handle by hand right now? I built ReviewAsk. It texts or emails clients a 1-tap review link after each job or closing, for $39/mo. Glad to set up a free trial if it's useful.

**If they ask "how does it work":**
> You add a customer's name and phone after the job. They get one text: "Thanks for choosing {{business}}! Mind leaving a quick review? {{link}}". One reminder 2 days later, then it stops. You see the new reviews on the dashboard.

## Instagram (salons / barbers), optional
> Love your work! Quick question: do you ask clients for Google reviews? I built a tool that texts them a link right after their appointment. Want to try it free?
