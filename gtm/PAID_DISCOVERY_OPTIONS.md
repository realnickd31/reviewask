# Refilling the lead pipeline: paid and manual options (for Nick, morning of Oct 27)
Nothing here has been bought, signed up for, or spent. Prices aren't listed on purpose: check each vendor's current pricing page before deciding. Effort estimates are rough [SPEC].

The free sources (Jobber Sites, Jobber Client Hub links, Housecall Pro booking pages on Nextdoor and Exa) are mostly used up: the last 5 batches found 3–8 new leads each.

## Suggested first move
Try #1 by hand for an hour in 3 states we haven't covered well (OR, WA, NM, ME, WI, IA), and look at #2's free tier for Jobber detection. Both cost nothing. Decide on #3 only if those turn up too few leads.

Whatever the source, every new lead still goes through the same rules: verify the count on a mirror or Maps before scoring Hot, exclude franchise and marketplace counts from comps, and keep outreach as drafts until you approve each send.
