## 1. Jobber Community (community.getjobber.com): reply or post in a reviews/marketing thread

**Title:** A simple Google-review ask routine that works with any Jobber plan

> A few things that make the biggest difference to how many customers actually leave a Google review:
>
> 1. **Ask the same day the job is closed.** The visit is still fresh, and a week later most people have forgotten.
> 2. **Use the direct review link.** In your Google Business Profile, click "Ask for reviews" and copy the link. It opens the review box straight away, so the customer doesn't have to search for you on Maps.
> 3. **Keep the message short:**
>    "Hi {first name}, thanks for having us out today! If we did a good job, would you leave a quick Google review? {link} – {your name}"
> 4. **Reply to every review**, good or bad. Future customers read the replies.
> 5. **Slow season:** send that same message once to everyone you served this year who hasn't reviewed you yet.
>
> You can do all of this by hand from Jobber's client list. Jobber's own automated review requests come with the Marketing Suite add-on (see https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/ for current pricing).
>

## 2. Facebook groups (Jobber users, lawn care, cleaning, pressure washing owner groups)

> Quick review tip for anyone stuck at 20–40 Google reviews while the shop down the road has 200:
>
> Most happy customers will leave a review. The trouble is that nobody asks them at the right moment. What tends to work:
> • Ask the **same day** you finish, by text if you have their OK to text, otherwise by email
> • Send the **direct review link** from your Google Business Profile ("Ask for reviews" button), not "search us on Google"
> • One short line, without "if you have a minute":
> "Hi {name}, thanks for having us out today! If we did a good job, would you leave a quick Google review? {link} – {owner}"
>
> Off-season bonus: send it once to everyone you served this year who hasn't reviewed you yet.
>
> Happy to explain how to find your direct review link if anyone's stuck.

## 3. Reddit (r/lawncare, r/pressurewashing, r/sweatystartup, r/smallbusiness): original post

**Title:** How a small service business can close a big Google-review gap (math + a template)

> The math: say the top 3 shops in your town average 180 reviews and you have 40. That's a gap of 140. Only a fraction of customers you ask will actually leave a review. If it's around 1 in 10 (track your own number), you need about 1,400 asks to catch up. That's why asking now and then never closes the gap, and asking after *every* job plus one past-customer blast does.
>
> What works:
> 1. Ask the same day the job is done
> 2. Use the direct "write a review" link from your Google Business Profile
> 3. If customers have agreed to texts, text tends to get noticed more than email
> 4. Reply to every review
> 5. In the off-season, send one message to past customers who haven't reviewed you yet
>
> Template: "Hi {name}, thanks for having us out today! If we did a good job, would you leave a quick Google review? {link} – {owner}"
>
> Google's rules: ask everyone, don't offer anything in exchange for reviews, and don't only ask the happy customers ("review gating").
>
> Disclosure: I run ReviewAsk, which automates this for Jobber users, but everything above works by hand.
