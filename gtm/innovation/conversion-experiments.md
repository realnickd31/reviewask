# Conversion experiments (ranked by expected impact ÷ effort). Website agent implements.
Low traffic means sequential tests (about 2 weeks each) or a "big-change" approach. Do not wait for statistical significance on small tweaks. Primary metric: visit → trial start. Secondary: trial → paid.

| # | Experiment | Hypothesis | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero: "Get more Google reviews from every Jobber & Housecall Pro job, $39/mo" + logos of both | Naming the tool gives instant relevance | High | Low |
| 2 | No credit card for the 7-day trial (vs card required) | More trials. Check trial→paid holds ≥ 40% of the lift | High | Med (billing) |
| 3 | Embedded review-gap mini-calculator above the fold (your count, competitor count → asks/mo) | Personal numbers create urgency and capture email | High | Med |
| 4 | Price anchor table: Marketing Suite / Podium / NiceJob vs $39 (cited, dated) | Makes $39 feel like an easy yes | Med-High | Low |
| 5 | CTA copy: "Start free trial" vs "Connect Jobber (free 7 days)" | Integration-first CTA fits marketplace intent | Med | Low |
| 6 | Signup: email + password only, then connect FSM, vs a long form | Fewer fields means more starts | Med | Low |
| 7 | Show the actual SMS/email the customer receives (phone mockup) | Takes away the fear of "what will my customers see" | Med | Low |
| 8 | Founder note + 10-min setup call offer | Small trades owners buy from people | Med | Low |
| 9 | "Ask last season's customers" backlog CTA (fall/winter seasonal banner) | Concrete win now | Med | Low |
| 10 | Exit intent: "Get your free review-gap audit" | Captures leads who aren't ready to buy | Low-Med | Low |

Instrument first: GA4/Plausible events for cta_click, signup_start, signup_complete, fsm_connected, first_ask_sent, paid.
