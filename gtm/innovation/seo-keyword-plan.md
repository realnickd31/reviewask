## Page outlines
**/jobber-marketing-suite-alternative**: H1 "A $39 Jobber Marketing Suite alternative for Google reviews". Who it's for (you only need review asks). Price table (Marketing Suite's add-on price, cited and dated from Jobber's pricing page, vs $39 flat). How it connects (Jobber job closed → ask). "When Marketing Suite is the better pick" (you also want campaigns and referrals). FAQ. CTA: 7-day trial.
**/jobber-google-reviews**: How to request reviews in Jobber natively vs automated. 3-step setup with screenshots. Template messages. Timing data. CTA.
**/housecall-pro-google-reviews**: Same structure; HCP's built-in review features (cite them), where ReviewAsk is simpler or cheaper, and setup.
**/nicejob-alternative, /podium-alternative**: Honest table with prices cited and dated, "pick them if…", migration in 10 minutes.
**/reviews-for-{trade}**: Trade-specific timing (e.g. pool: after the first clean of the season), a template, a mini review-gap example, CTA to the free audit.
**/templates/review-request**: 10 copy-paste SMS/email templates; email gate optional.
**/tools/review-link**: Place ID → `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=…`; offer "auto-send this after every job".

## Rules
One page per intent. Cite competitor prices with a date. Include "when they're better" sections (builds trust and helps rank for comparison queries). Internal-link everything to the audit and the trial. Ship #1 and #2 first.
