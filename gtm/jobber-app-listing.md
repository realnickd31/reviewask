## App description (short — one line)
Get more 5-star Google reviews: ReviewAsk asks each customer for a review when you close their job in Jobber.

## App description (long)
Happy customers rarely leave reviews unless someone asks — and after a long day on the truck, nobody remembers to ask.

ReviewAsk does the asking for you. Connect your Jobber account once and turn on "Ask after every job." From then on, when a job is closed in Jobber, ReviewAsk sends that customer a short, friendly message from your business with a one-tap button that opens your Google review page. If they don't get to it, ReviewAsk sends one polite reminder a few days later, then stops.

You don't need new habits or new software for your crew. Keep running jobs in Jobber the way you already do. ReviewAsk works in the background and shows you every request it sent in one simple dashboard.

Built for home-service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning, landscaping and more.

## Features or benefits
- Automatic review requests when a job is closed in Jobber — no extra clicks for you or your techs
- One-tap button that takes customers straight to your Google review page
- One polite reminder, then it stops — no spamming your customers
- Messages go out under your business name
- Simple dashboard that shows every request and reminder sent
- Ask on the spot from your phone, or paste a list of past customers
- Customers can unsubscribe with one click
- Disconnect any time from ReviewAsk Settings or from Jobber

## Before you start (configure after submitting)
- You'll need a ReviewAsk account (free trial). If you don't have one, you'll be asked to create one when you click Connect — it takes about a minute.
- Have your Google review link handy (in Google Business Profile, click **Ask for reviews** and copy the link).
- Make sure your Jobber clients have an email address or mobile number on file — that's where the review request goes.
